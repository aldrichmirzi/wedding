Frequently Asked Questions

◆
The dress code is Formal Attire. We warmly encourage our guests to wear our wedding colors — Burgundy and Emerald Green. Out of respect for the bride, kindly avoid wearing white or ivory. Please also avoid sleeveless attire inside the church.
Yes! Parking is available at Palmeras Private Resort for the reception. We recommend arriving a little early to secure a spot. Carpooling with fellow guests is also a wonderful idea.
We kindly request strictly no plus ones. Due to limited seating and venue capacity, only the guests named on the invitation will be accommodated. We truly appreciate your understanding! 🙏
We adore your little ones and they are most welcome at both the ceremony and reception! We just kindly ask parents/guardians to keep them close and well-behaved, especially inside the church, out of respect for the solemnity of the mass. Thank you so much for understanding! 🙏💚
Your presence truly is our greatest gift. Should you wish to give something, we humbly and gladly accept anything — big or small, near or far! Every gift is a blessing and we are grateful for all of it. 😄🙏
