How It All Began

Our Story

◆
💌

Our story began long before we knew it would. We were batchmates in elementary, though we never spoke to each other back then. After graduation, we each went our separate ways in high school, never imagining that our paths would cross again almost ten years later.

In 2018, a simple hello became the beginning of something neither of us expected. What began with a single message soon turned into countless late-night talks, laughter, and the surprising feeling that we had known each other for much longer than we actually had. Somewhere between those conversations, we fell in love.

◆

Over the years, we have shared countless beautiful memories, grown together, and learned that love is not only about the happiest moments, but also about choosing each other through life's most difficult seasons. We have experienced joy, faced challenges, and endured a heartbreaking loss that brought us even closer and reminded us how precious it is to have someone to hold on to. Through it all, we found in each other a best friend, a safe place, and a partner for life.

What began unexpectedly became the love we now know we never want to live without. Looking back, we believe our story was never simply about finding each other — it was about finding our way back to each other at exactly the right time. And today, as we begin this new chapter, we are certain that this is only the beginning of our forever.
